# Local alert sounds

Place short PCM WAV files in this directory on the ROCK 5B+.

Recommended names:

- `test.wav`
- `follow.wav`
- `sub.wav`
- `giftsub.wav`
- `raid.wav`
- `bits.wav`
- `channelpoint.wav`
- `moderator.wav`
- `system.wav`

Keep the sounds short and at a moderate volume. The Bluetooth speaker may be picked up by the DJI Mic, so the field alerts should normally be brief notifications rather than the full OBS alert audio.

Audio files are ignored by Git to avoid committing copyrighted or private media.
